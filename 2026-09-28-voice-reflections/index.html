Try it this week

Two short reflections to try.

Voice gives us another way to work with AI. We can speak naturally, explain what we mean, and ask a follow-up without first turning every thought into a carefully written prompt. Two short journals can show you what to keep doing and what to fix.

01
Set up

Two journals, kept apart.

Use two projects if your app offers them.

Set up two separate journals: Joy and Success, and Just FIXIT! If your app offers Projects and supports your voice workflow there, use two projects. Otherwise, keep two clearly named chats or two saved notes. Use dated entries, and keep personal reflections separate from work records.

At the weekly review, start a new chat in each project and ask which dates the AI can see. If any are missing, add them before you ask for patterns. Do not assume that a project name or memory feature gives it access to every entry.

02
Project one

Joy and Success.

Five minutes a day.

At the end of the day, talk about what went well, what felt successful, and what brought you joy or happiness. What helped those moments happen? Five minutes is plenty; take up to ten if you want. On a difficult day, there is no need to manufacture a positive moment.

Try saying

I want to add a Joy and Success entry for today. Ask me what went well, what felt successful, and what brought me joy, one question at a time. When we finish, give me a short dated summary I can keep.

Friday review

After Friday’s entry, start a new chat in the project and ask AI to reflect back what it notices. Treat its themes as possibilities.

Try saying

I’ve been journaling about what went well this week and want to think it through with you. What keeps showing up? Where did I light up? Point to specific entries, and end with one question that helps me see what I might be missing.

03
Project two

Just FIXIT!

No more than five minutes a day.

Describe what drained your energy or made the day harder. Perhaps it was expense reporting, repeated data entry, or another tedious task. What part caused the friction, and does it keep happening? Focus on the task and process, and leave out unnecessary personal details about other people.

Try saying

I want to add a Just FIXIT! entry for today. Ask me what drained my energy or made the day harder, and which part of the task caused the friction, one question at a time. When we finish, give me a short dated summary I can keep.

Friday review

A process problem may need a process change. The aim is to understand what would help, not to accept unnecessary frustration.

Try saying

I’ve been journaling about what got in my way this week. What keeps showing up? Which sticking points could be simplified, automated, delegated, or improved with AI, and where is AI not a good fit? Help me pick one small change to test next week.

Your invitationThis week

Choose one journal to begin. On Friday, ask yourself: What did voice help me capture? What needed correcting? What is one thing I want to keep doing?
