AI Tip of the Month

Turn Your Phone into a Mobile Thinking Partner

Capture what you learn in conversation, notice what your week is telling you, and reflect on it with AI while the details are fresh.

Capture Speak the details while they are fresh

Alumni Relations

Capture alumni stories

After a commencement or alumni event conversation, speak the story, the memorable quote, and any follow-up idea. Ask AI to shape it into a story lead, event recap, or newsletter item.

Corporate Outreach

Organize conference insights

After a booth conversation, dictate the prospect’s interests, open questions, and their own start timeframe. Ask AI for a lead summary and recommended next steps.

Business Development

Strengthen partnership follow-up

After a partner meeting, talk through opportunities, objections, decisions, and next steps. Ask AI for a meeting summary, action plan, or follow-up email.

Try It This Week 1Joy and Success2Just FIXIT!

  1. Use two projects to store your reflections.SET UP
    Create “Joy and Success” and “Just FIXIT!” and keep each entry in the matching project.
  2. Journal daily, or two to three times a week.NOTICE
    Talk for a few minutes: what went well and brought you joy, or what dragged and needs fixing.
  3. After Friday’s entry, start a new chat in each project.REFLECT
    Ask AI to review your reflections from the week and reflect back any helpful insights.
1Joy and Success: Friday prompt

“I’ve been journaling about what went well this week and want to think it through with you. What keeps showing up? Where did I light up? Point to specific entries, and end with one question that helps me see what I might be missing.”

2Just FIXIT! Friday prompt“I’ve been journaling about what got in my way this week. What keeps showing up? Which sticking points could be simplified, automated, delegated, or improved with AI, and where is AI not a good fit? Help me pick one small change to test next week.”
